# Mockup image provenance

The three blank product photographs in the brand book were generated with the built-in image generation tool. The Evolvate marks placed over them in PowerPoint are editable native shapes and text. These images are conceptual presentation mockups, not production proofs.

## Shirt

Premium photorealistic studio product image of a single forest-green cotton crew-neck T-shirt, front facing and laid completely flat, with a smooth blank chest for an editable logo overlay. Soft warm off-white background, diffused daylight, subtle fabric texture, refined Scandinavian styling. No text, logo, graphics, watermark, or extra objects.

## Notebook

Premium photorealistic studio product image of a closed A5 hardcover notebook with a warm cream cover, lying flat on a pale stone desk, viewed nearly top-down. Blank, unobstructed front cover for an editable logo overlay. Soft directional daylight and understated Scandinavian office mood. No text, logo, graphics, watermark, or extra objects.

## Pen

Premium photorealistic studio product image of one matte dark forest-green metal ballpoint pen placed horizontally on a warm off-white surface, side profile and large in frame. Blank visible barrel for an editable logo overlay. Restrained lighting and crisp product detail. No text, logo, graphics, watermark, or extra objects.
