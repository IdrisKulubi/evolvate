# Design System: Evolvate Consulting

## 1. Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Strategic Journal"**

Evolvate should feel like a beautifully edited strategy journal brought to life: considered, authoritative, and easy to navigate. Kinfolk supplies editorial restraint, Pentagram supplies identity rigor, and Apple supplies clarity. None is a template. Together they set an expectation of precise hierarchy, meaningful whitespace, and confidence without corporate theater.

The primary scene is a decision-maker reviewing Evolvate on a bright office display while weighing a consequential business choice. The visual system is therefore light-led, calm, and highly legible. Warm tinted neutrals create the atmosphere, while one distinctive accent guides attention sparingly. Occasional deep sections may create rhythm, but dark styling is not the default.

Motion is choreographed but disciplined. Entrances and selective scroll sequences should reveal structure, explain relationships, and reinforce brand recall. Content must never wait for animation, and reduced-motion behavior is a first-class expression of the same design.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Editorial hierarchy with composed, asymmetrical layouts
- Warm, light-led surfaces with one rare brand accent
- Premium serif display typography paired with a precise sans-serif
- Purposeful choreography with fast, readable fallbacks
- Clear conversion paths centered on consultation

**The Approved Mark Rule.** Final color values must be resolved from the approved Evolvate logo before implementation is treated as complete.

## 3. Typography

**Display Font:** Editorial serif `[font family to be chosen at implementation]`  
**Body Font:** Precise sans-serif `[font family to be chosen at implementation]`

**Character:** The serif brings judgment, authority, and an editorial cadence to major statements. The sans-serif keeps navigation, explanatory copy, forms, and calls to action direct and highly legible.

### Hierarchy
- **Display:** High contrast and tightly composed, reserved for hero statements and defining section ideas.
- **Headline:** At least 1.25 times the next step, used for major content transitions.
- **Title:** Decisive and compact, used for services, insights, and process steps.
- **Body:** Calm, readable, and capped at 65 to 75 characters per line.
- **Label:** Concise and precise, used for navigation, metadata, and controls without excessive uppercase styling.

**The Editorial Contrast Rule.** Use the serif for perspective and the sans-serif for action. Do not alternate them decoratively.

**The Fewer, Stronger Sizes Rule.** Establish hierarchy through meaningful jumps in scale and weight. Tiny incremental size changes are forbidden.

## 4. Elevation

The system is flat by default. Depth comes from tonal shifts, overlap, scale, and motion rather than decorative shadows. A restrained ambient lift may appear when an interactive element responds to hover or focus, but elevation must communicate state or hierarchy.

**The Earned Elevation Rule.** A shadow is permitted only when it explains interaction or spatial order. If a surface looks like a floating template card at rest, remove the shadow.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use warm, light-led surfaces for the bright-office viewing context.
- **Do** reserve the single Evolvate accent for primary actions and brand-defining moments.
- **Do** use editorial composition, intentional whitespace, and asymmetry to make information easier to scan.
- **Do** pair every choreographed sequence with an immediately readable static state.
- **Do** respect reduced-motion preferences and preserve full keyboard, screen-reader, contrast, and touch accessibility.
- **Do** keep consultation as the dominant conversion action.
- **Do** use specific outcomes and practical language to express authority.

### Don't:
- **Don't** resemble a Big Four consulting template with corporate blue, stock handshakes, or dense service tiles.
- **Don't** use a cheap template appearance or allow prebuilt components to dictate the brand identity.
- **Don't** build a stock-photo-heavy page.
- **Don't** repeat excessive card grids or nest cards inside cards.
- **Don't** use overused gradient blobs, gradient text, cartoon-style illustration, or decorative glassmorphism.
- **Don't** create cluttered layouts, unnecessary popups, or competing primary actions.
- **Don't** use decorative motion, constant cursor tricks, or effects that delay or obscure content.
- **Don't** use excessive corporate jargon, empty buzzwords, generic claims, long paragraphs, or unsupported superlatives.
- **Don't** use colored side-stripe borders as accents.
- **Don't** sacrifice readability, usability, performance, or trust for visual novelty.
