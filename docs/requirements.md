 # Evolvate Consulting Website — Product Requirements Document

**Document:** `requirements.md`  
**Client:** Evolvate Consulting  
**Location:** Luleå, Sweden  
**Project Type:** Premium Consulting Website  
**Primary Goal:** Build a modern, premium, highly animated consulting website that establishes trust quickly, communicates Evolvate's value clearly, and converts visitors into qualified consultation leads.

---

## 1. Business Context

Evolvate Consulting is a consulting firm focused on helping businesses grow, improve operational efficiency, and gain stronger financial and project-management control.

The business plan positions Evolvate around three core consulting areas:

1. **Business Development Consulting**
   - Market research and analysis
   - Growth strategies
   - Competitive positioning
   - Business planning

2. **Financial Management Consulting**
   - Budgeting
   - Cost management
   - Financial forecasting
   - Project accounting

3. **Project Management Consulting**
   - Strategic planning
   - Resource allocation
   - Risk management

The company intends to serve:
- Startups
- Small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs)
- Established corporations

The website should therefore feel credible enough for corporate decision-makers while still being approachable for founders and growing SMEs.

---

# 2. Website Vision

The Evolvate website should NOT look like a generic consulting template.

The intended experience is:

> **Premium strategy consultancy meets modern technology company.**

The website should feel:
- Sophisticated
- Intelligent
- Confident
- Minimal
- Global
- Modern
- Strategic
- Trustworthy
- Highly polished
- Conversion-focused

The visual experience should communicate competence before the visitor reads every word.

The design should use deliberate motion, strong typography, premium spacing, intelligent content hierarchy, modern interactions, and subtle visual storytelling.

The objective is not animation for decoration.

**Every visual effect must improve one of these outcomes:**
1. Attention
2. Understanding
3. Trust
4. Brand recall
5. Conversion

---

# 3. Core Website Objectives

The website must:

- Establish Evolvate Consulting as a credible professional consultancy.
- Clearly explain the company's consulting services.
- Communicate the company's value proposition.
- Encourage prospective clients to request an initial consultation.
- Support the company's digital marketing strategy.
- Support search engine visibility.
- Build authority through insights, case studies, and thought leadership.
- Make it easy for visitors to understand which service is relevant to them.
- Generate qualified leads.
- Support future expansion into additional services, markets, and content.
- Present Evolvate as a modern consultancy rather than a traditional static consulting firm.

# 26. Motion & Animation System

Animation should be one of the signature aspects of the website.

## 26.1 Page Load
Use:
- Elegant logo reveal
- Short typography transition
- Hero content stagger
- Visual reveal

Do NOT make users wait through a long intro animation.

Target:
**Initial reveal under approximately 1 second after content is ready.**

## 26.2 Scroll Animations
Use:
- Fade + translate
- Staggered text
- Masked text reveals
- Image reveals
- Number / metric animation
- Section transitions
- Horizontal motion where meaningful
- Scroll-linked process storytelling

Avoid making every element move.

## 26.3 Text Animation
Headline options:
- Line-by-line reveal
- Word mask reveal
- Character animation only for short branded statements
- Text color transitions tied to scroll

Ensure readable fallback when JavaScript is unavailable.

## 26.4 Interactive Service Cards
On hover:
- Slight elevation
- Micro-parallax
- Background shift
- Icon movement
- Arrow motion

Animations should feel controlled and high-end.

## 26.5 Cursor Interactions
Desktop-only optional feature:
- Links
- Case studies
- Service cards
- Draggable experiences

Must gracefully disappear on touch devices.

## 26.6 Smooth Scrolling
Use subtle smooth scrolling only if:
- Accessibility is preserved
- Native browser behavior is not broken
- Performance remains excellent

## 26.7 Microinteractions
Include microinteractions for:
- Buttons
- Navigation
- Form focus
- Form validation
- Accordions
- Dropdowns
- Links
- Loading states
- Success states

---

# 27. Animation Accessibility

Respect:

```css
@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)
```

Users requesting reduced motion should receive:
- Minimal transitions
- No strong parallax
- No disorienting scroll-linked effects

5. What should I do next?

And the visitor can reach a consultation request with minimal friction.

---

# 69. Primary UX Principle

> **Clarity first. Motion second. Conversion always.**

The most impressive website is not the one with the most animation.

It is the website where premium visual design, storytelling, speed, trust and motion work together so the visitor feels:

> "These people understand serious business — I want to talk to them."

# 70. Final Experience Standard

The final Evolvate website should feel more like a modern global consultancy / strategy-tech company than a traditional small-business consulting template.

Every page should demonstrate:
- Precision
- Confidence
- Business intelligence
- High-end visual craft
- Smooth interactions
- Strong messaging
- Clear conversion paths

The experience should remain memorable without sacrificing usability, accessibility, SEO, or loading speed.
